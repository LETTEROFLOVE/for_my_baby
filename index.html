8th of August

♛

To My Queen, Tanisha,

❦ ♥ ❦

Distance has a strange way of making love louder. Every mile between us seems to echo back the same thing — that you are the person I choose, again and again, even from here. I don't get to hold your hand today, so let me hold it in ink instead.

You are the first thing I think of before I've even opened my eyes properly, and the last thought I keep tucked away before I sleep. I call the space between us "Tanyam" sometimes — half you, half me, stitched into one word, the way I wish we could always be stitched into one room.

I know I can't close the distance yet. But I can give you something steadier than a promise made of words alone — a promise I intend to keep with my actions, one ordinary day at a time.

♛ By Royal Decree ♛

I, Sanyam, your King though I stand a kingdom away, do hereby vow to you, Tanisha, my Queen: from the 8th of August to the 22nd of August, no cigarette shall touch my hand, and no drink shall touch my lips. Not one, not even "just this once" — fifteen days, whole and unbroken, sworn on my crown.

A king's word is his throne — worth nothing if it cannot bear weight. So I do not offer you flowers that wilt, nor promises that fade by morning. I offer you discipline, chosen freely, for the love of my Queen, and for the man I want to be when I am finally beside you again.

Let this decree stand as my bond. Question me. Hold me to account. When the 22nd arrives, let it be known that every one of those fifteen days was ruled, quietly and completely, in your name.

Hold me to it, my Queen. Ask me every day if you must — I welcome it. And when the 22nd comes, know that every single one of those days was governed by you, and you alone.

Yours, in this life and every distance between us,

Sanyam

Your King
