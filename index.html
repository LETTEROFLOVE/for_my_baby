8th of August

My Dearest Tanisha,

Distance has a strange way of making love louder. Every mile between us seems to echo back the same thing — that you are the person I choose, again and again, even from here. I don't get to hold your hand today, so let me hold it in ink instead.

You are the first thing I think of before I've even opened my eyes properly, and the last thought I keep tucked away before I sleep. I call the space between us "Tanyam" sometimes — half you, half me, stitched into one word, the way I wish we could always be stitched into one room.

I know I can't close the distance yet. But I can give you something steadier than a promise made of words alone — a promise I intend to keep with my actions, one ordinary day at a time.

The Promise

From the 8th of August to the 22nd of August, I will not smoke. I will not drink. Not one cigarette, not one drink — for fifteen days, for you, and honestly, for me too.

Every morning I wake up and keep this is a small proof, sent across the miles, that you make me want to be someone better.

Hold me to it. Ask me every day if you have to — I want you to. And when the 22nd comes, I want you to know that every single one of those days had your name on it.

Forever, and from every distance,

Sanyam
